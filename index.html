Philadelphia-based backend engineer with a passion for building systems that scale. Since graduating from Drexel University in 2019, I've crafted robust backend solutions at industry leaders like Comcast and FanDuel.

My journey began at Comcast, engineering internal web platforms and an intelligent chatbot that helped customers resolve issues quickly. Now at FanDuel, I've grown from SEO optimization challenges to partnership integrations that power critical business relationships.

Java/Spring Boot is my go-to stack, though I enjoy exploring new technologies and mentoring junior developers. I believe in writing clean, maintainable code that delivers real business value.

Outside of work, you'll find me rooting for Philadelphia sports teams, spending time with family and friends, or hanging out with my dog, Ralphie.
